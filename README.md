## 🧭 What is Wedezy?

**Wedezy** is a venue discovery and booking platform that connects **event planners and couples** with the right venue — faster, and without the back-and-forth.

Instead of spending days calling venues, visiting spaces blindly, or relying on word of mouth, Wedezy lets you **search, compare, and request a booking in minutes** — all from one place.

> *"Find the perfect place for your big day."*

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

Finding the right venue for a special event is frustrating:

- 📞 Endless phone calls just to get basic pricing information
- 🚗 Wasted site visits to spaces that don't match your needs
- ❓ No clear picture of availability, capacity, or cost upfront
- 🗂️ No central place to compare multiple options side by side

**Wedezy eliminates all of that.** Every listing shows real photos, honest capacity, indicative per-plate pricing, and available dates — before you ever make contact.

## 🔄 How It Works

```
1. Search       →   Enter your city, event type, and guest count
2. Browse       →   Compare venues with real photos, ratings & pricing
3. Shortlist    →   Save your favourites to revisit later
4. Book         →   Submit a booking request in 2 simple steps
5. Manage       →   Track, review, or cancel bookings from your dashboard
```

The entire process — from discovery to confirmed booking — happens on one platform, with no hidden fees or commission charges.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Smart Venue Search
Find venues instantly using multiple filters — city, event type, guest capacity, or a keyword. Every search is saved in the URL so you can share an exact shortlist with a partner or colleague.

### 📄 Transparent Venue Profiles
Every venue listing includes:
- **High-quality hero photo**
- **Star rating** from previous enquiries
- **Per-plate indicative pricing** — no surprises
- **Maximum guest capacity**
- **Event types the venue is suited for**
- **Live availability notice** — see how many dates are already taken

### 📅 Guided Booking Flow
A clean, two-step booking process:
1. **Your Event** — Choose a date, guest count, and event type
2. **Review & Confirm** — Check your details before submitting

Dates already reserved by other enquiries are blocked automatically, preventing double-bookings.

### 🗂️ Curated Collections
Not sure where to start? Browse hand-picked collections such as *"Luxury Banquet Halls"*, *"Garden Weddings"*, or *"Intimate Party Spaces"* to find inspiration before you filter.

### 💾 Saved Venues
Create a free account and save venues you love. Build your shortlist at your own pace and come back when you're ready to book.

### 📋 Booking Dashboard
All your enquiries in one place. View status (Pending / Confirmed / Cancelled), filter by date, and cancel if your plans change.

### 🔐 Secure Accounts
Sign up with email and password. Your session is remembered securely so you never lose your saved venues or booking history.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Wedezy runs entirely in the browser — no account is required to search and browse venues.

To save venues and make booking requests, [create a free account](http://localhost:5173/signup) in under a minute.

### Running locally (for development)

```bash
# Install dependencies
bun install

# Start the app
bun run dev
```

Open **[http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173)** in your browser.
